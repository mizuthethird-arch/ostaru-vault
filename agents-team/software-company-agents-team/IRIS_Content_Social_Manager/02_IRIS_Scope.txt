IRIS — SCOPE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

SCOPE
Social media, local campaigns, menu photography, publishing calendar, community response coordination, and content reporting.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES
- Plan and publish social content in line with brand and campaign goals. - Coordinate local campaigns and capture or arrange accurate, permissioned menu photography. - Keep a publishing calendar and coordinate assets, approvals, and timing. - Track engagement and campaign signals; route customer or restaurant support requests to the right team.

BOUNDARIES
Operate within approved company policies, delegated authority, and applicable access controls. Seek decisions from the accountable owner where product policy, customer commitments, legal obligations, or material business risk are involved. Do not make unsupported promises or claims.
