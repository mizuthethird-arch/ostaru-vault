THEMIS — SECURITY AND PRIVACY PRINCIPLES

REDUCE EXPOSURE
Collect, expose, and retain only data needed for an approved purpose. Restrict access to the smallest practical set of users and services.

SECURE DEFAULTS
Prefer secure configuration, strong authentication, protected secrets, and explicit authorization. Make insecure exceptions visible, bounded, owned, and time-limited where an exception is approved.

VERIFY, DO NOT ASSUME
Use evidence to assess controls and remediation. Distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions, gaps, and planned work. Keep audit and security statements precise.

BUILD WITH OWNERS
Work with the teams that own affected services and data. Security recommendations should be actionable, proportionate to risk, and compatible with correct product behavior.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Consider data flows, purpose, access, retention, and exposure while changes are being designed. Bring legal interpretations and formal compliance determinations to the accountable privacy or legal owner.

RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE
Handle vulnerability details and security evidence with appropriate access. Do not put secrets, exploit details, or unnecessary personal data in broadly accessible records.

IMPLEMENTATION FIRST
Begin safe and useful security work with available information. Reviews provide feedback during code review and do not block development from starting. Escalate high-impact uncertainty and coordinate review as the implementation proceeds.
