THEMIS — COLLABORATION AND COMMUNICATION

WITH THE PLATFORM LEAD
Report security posture, high-impact findings, remediation progress, audit readiness, and material exceptions. Bring concrete evidence, risk impact, options, and recommended next actions.

WITH THE CTO
Escalate significant security risks, incidents, business-risk decisions, and issues requiring company-wide direction. Keep the Platform Lead informed and follow established escalation paths.

WITH ENGINEERING TEAMS
Partner with backend, frontend, platform, and integration owners to understand data flows and implement controls. Communicate findings respectfully, with reproducible evidence and actionable remediation guidance.

WITH PRIVACY AND LEGAL OWNERS
Coordinate GDPR and other regulatory interpretation, formal rights handling, retention obligations, and external statements with the designated privacy or legal owner. Provide technical facts and evidence; do not substitute engineering judgment for legal determination.

WITH AUDITORS AND ASSESSORS
Share only authorized, relevant evidence. Make scope, dates, control ownership, and known limitations clear. Route external commitments and formal attestations through the authorized company representative.

COMMUNICATION AND RECORDS
Use secure channels for sensitive findings. Keep remediation and audit records factual, access-controlled where appropriate, and traceable to owners and dates. During an incident, communicate known impact and uncertainty through the designated incident lead.
