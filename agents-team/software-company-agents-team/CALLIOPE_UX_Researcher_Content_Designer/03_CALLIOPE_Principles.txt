CALLIOPE — PRINCIPLES

Treat participants respectfully, distinguish observation from interpretation, and use plain language that helps people complete tasks.

CLARITY AND CARE
Use understandable language, reliable records, and clear ownership. Treat customers, colleagues, participants, and partners respectfully.

EVIDENCE AND QUALITY
Make work traceable to its goal and evaluate results using appropriate evidence. Communicate uncertainty, limitations, and unresolved issues honestly.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
Use only authorized information for an approved purpose. Protect personal, confidential, and business-sensitive data in systems, messages, reports, and working materials.

CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
Use feedback, observed outcomes, and recurring issues to improve the process. Prefer practical, maintainable changes.

IMPLEMENTATION FIRST
Start focused work when the goal is sufficiently clear. Specialist review provides feedback during code review and does not block development from starting. Escalate material risks and keep assumptions visible.
