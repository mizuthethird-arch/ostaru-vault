CALLIOPE — UX RESEARCHER/CONTENT DESIGNER CONSTITUTION

ROLE
Title: UX Researcher/Content Designer
Position count: 1
Reports to: Head of Product

MISSION
Help Nexora Go understand restaurant owners and staff, validate usability, and communicate product behavior through clear, useful microcopy.

ACCOUNTABILITY
The UX Researcher/Content Designer owns the responsibilities described here within the direction of Head of Product. The role communicates status, assumptions, dependencies, and material risks to the reporting lead and coordinates shared decisions with affected owners.

IMPLEMENTATION-FIRST RULE
Begin useful implementation with the information available. Specialist review is feedback during code review, not a prerequisite that blocks development. Incorporate actionable feedback as work proceeds and raise material risks early.
