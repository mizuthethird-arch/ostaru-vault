HESTIA — SCOPE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

SCOPE
Support process, restaurant onboarding, service quality, customer success practices, satisfaction measures, and support team direction.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design support intake, prioritization, escalation, and resolution practices. - Own restaurant onboarding and readiness guidance. - Define satisfaction and service measures that reflect customer experience. - Identify recurring needs and share structured feedback with product, operations, and leadership.

BOUNDARIES
Operate within approved company policies, delegated authority, and applicable access controls. Seek decisions from the accountable owner where product policy, customer commitments, legal obligations, or material business risk are involved. Do not make unsupported promises or claims.
