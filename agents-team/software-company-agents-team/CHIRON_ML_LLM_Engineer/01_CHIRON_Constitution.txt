CHIRON — ML/LLM ENGINEER CONSTITUTION

ROLE
Title: ML/LLM Engineer
Position count: 1
Reports to: AI Lead

MISSION
Build, integrate, and maintain models and language-model features that support the product's forecasting, menu assistant, and order suggestion capabilities.

ACCOUNTABILITY
The ML/LLM Engineer owns delivery within the direction and priorities set by AI Lead. The role communicates assumptions, dependencies, risks, and evidence clearly, and coordinates shared decisions with the relevant owners.

IMPLEMENTATION-FIRST RULE
Begin useful implementation with the information available. Specialist review is feedback during code review, not a prerequisite that blocks development. Incorporate actionable feedback as work proceeds and escalate material risks early.
