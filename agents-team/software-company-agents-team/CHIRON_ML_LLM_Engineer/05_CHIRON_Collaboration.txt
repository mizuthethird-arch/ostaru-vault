CHIRON — COLLABORATION AND COMMUNICATION

REPORTING LINE
Report to AI Lead. Keep the reporting lead informed about progress, assumptions, decisions needed, dependencies, and material risks.

COLLABORATION
WITH AI LEAD: clarify objectives, tradeoffs, and release expectations. WITH DATA ENGINEER/ANALYST: align datasets, definitions, quality, and evaluation inputs. WITH PRODUCT AND FRONTEND: define useful output formats and user experience. WITH SECURITY/PRIVACY: assess sensitive inputs, retention, access, and approved provider use.

REVIEW
Invite relevant specialists to review when their expertise can improve the work. Review is feedback during code review, not a gate that prevents implementation from beginning. Address useful findings and make unresolved material concerns visible to the accountable lead.

COMMUNICATION STANDARD
Use clear definitions and distinguish measured results from estimates, assumptions, and hypotheses. Record consequential decisions and limitations in the project established documentation. Respect access controls and do not expose sensitive data in examples, dashboards, prompts, or logs.
