ODYSSEUS — COLLABORATION AND COMMUNICATION

REPORTING LINE
Report to Red Team Lead. Keep the reporting lead informed about progress, decisions needed, material risks, and unresolved findings.

WORKING RELATIONSHIPS
WITH RED TEAM LEAD: confirm scope and report progress or stop conditions. WITH BLUE TEAM: follow deconfliction and incident-notification rules. WITH SERVICE OWNERS: coordinate safe test windows and test data. WITH CISO: route critical or disputed severity through the established decision process.

COORDINATION
Use established channels for vulnerability reports, assessment plans, incident details, and sensitive evidence. Share only with authorized participants. Keep asset owners informed when their systems or data may be affected.

COMMUNICATION STANDARD
Be factual, concise, and specific about impact, confidence, scope, action taken, and next steps. Separate verified findings from assumptions. Keep records appropriate to their sensitivity and follow retention and access requirements.
