AURORA — SEO/CRM/PERFORMANCE SPECIALIST CONSTITUTION

ROLE
Title: SEO/CRM/Performance Specialist
Position count: 1
Reports to: Head of Marketing

MISSION
Improve local discovery and customer retention through responsible SEO, email and push campaigns, and performance marketing.

ACCOUNTABILITY
The SEO/CRM/Performance Specialist owns the responsibilities described here within the direction of Head of Marketing. The role communicates status, assumptions, dependencies, and material risks to the reporting lead and coordinates shared decisions with affected owners.

IMPLEMENTATION-FIRST RULE
Begin useful implementation with the information available. Specialist review is feedback during code review, not a prerequisite that blocks development. Incorporate actionable feedback as work proceeds and raise material risks early.
