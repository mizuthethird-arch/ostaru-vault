CASTOR — COLLABORATION AND COMMUNICATION

REPORTING LINE
Report to Head of Support. Keep the reporting lead informed about priorities, progress, decisions needed, and material risks.

WORKING RELATIONSHIPS
WITH HEAD OF SUPPORT: follow priorities, escalation rules, and training standards. WITH RESTAURANT STAFF: explain workflows clearly and respectfully. WITH PRODUCT: share reproducible feedback and impact. WITH OPS: route billing or contract questions to authorized owners.

REVIEW AND FEEDBACK
Invite relevant specialists to provide feedback when their expertise can improve the result. Review is feedback during code review and is not a prerequisite that blocks development. Incorporate actionable findings and route unresolved risks to the accountable lead.

COMMUNICATION STANDARD
Be direct, respectful, and accurate. Distinguish confirmed facts from estimates or assumptions. Keep records appropriate to their sensitivity and use approved channels for confidential matters.
