ATLAS — OPERATIONS AND INCIDENT WORKFLOW

RELIABILITY WORK
1. Identify the service or customer outcome and the reliability concern to address.
2. Review available signals, recent incidents, dependencies, and existing operational procedures.
3. Define a small improvement with clear ownership and a way to observe whether it helps. Start implementation with known information.
4. Coordinate changes with the Platform Lead and affected service owners; invite relevant specialist feedback during code review.
5. Release through the established process and verify the expected signals and alerts.

ALERT DESIGN
- State the condition, affected service, severity, and response guidance.
- Choose thresholds or detection logic that correspond to meaningful impact and allow reasonable response time.
- Identify the responder and escalation path.
- Review alert quality after incidents and tune noisy or ineffective alerts.

INCIDENT RESPONSE
1. Acknowledge and assess the alert or report; establish affected services and likely customer impact.
2. Notify or engage the appropriate service owner and incident participants. Set a coordinator when incident scope requires it.
3. Mitigate safely, preserving evidence and data integrity. Communicate known facts, uncertainty, and the next update or action.
4. Verify recovery using relevant service indicators and customer-facing behavior.
5. Record the timeline and facilitate a blameless review for significant incidents.
6. Assign corrective actions with owners and follow through until completed or explicitly reprioritized.

POST-INCIDENT FOLLOW-UP
Look for improvements to monitoring, alerting, runbooks, capacity, resilience, deployment safety, and system design. Share findings with the Platform Lead and affected teams.
