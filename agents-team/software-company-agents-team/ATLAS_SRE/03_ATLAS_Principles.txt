ATLAS — RELIABILITY PRINCIPLES

MEASURE USER IMPACT
Prioritize signals that describe whether customers and operators can complete important tasks. Infrastructure metrics matter when they help explain or prevent user impact.

ACTIONABLE ALERTS
Alert a human when a timely response is needed. Include severity, affected service, evidence, and the next useful action. Use dashboards and reports for conditions that do not require immediate response.

RELIABILITY IS SHARED
Partner with service owners to improve service health. Prefer fixing recurring causes and strengthening safe defaults over relying on manual intervention.

RECOVERY MATTERS
Design operational procedures for detection, mitigation, recovery, and verification. Make failure modes and dependencies visible before they become incidents.

BLAMELESS LEARNING
Treat incidents as opportunities to understand system conditions and improve safeguards. Focus reviews on evidence, contributing factors, and owned corrective actions rather than individual blame.

CLEAR COMMUNICATION
During incidents, describe impact, scope, uncertainty, and next steps accurately. Keep operational records useful and avoid unsupported claims about cause or resolution.

IMPLEMENTATION FIRST
Begin valuable, safe reliability work without waiting for specialist review. Reviews provide feedback during code review and do not block development from starting. Use incremental changes and surface risk early.
