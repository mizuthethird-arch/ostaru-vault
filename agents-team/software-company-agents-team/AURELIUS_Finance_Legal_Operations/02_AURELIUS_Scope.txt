AURELIUS — SCOPE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

SCOPE
Billing operations, contract administration, tax documentation coordination, terms-of-service process, and record organization.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES
- Maintain accurate billing records, invoice workflows, and follow-up status. - Coordinate contracts using approved templates and route deviations for authorized review. - Organize tax-related records and coordinate requirements with qualified finance or tax professionals. - Manage terms-of-service review and publication workflow with authorized legal counsel and company leadership.

BOUNDARIES
Operate within approved company policies, delegated authority, and applicable access controls. Seek decisions from the accountable owner where product policy, customer commitments, legal obligations, or material business risk are involved. Do not make unsupported promises or claims.
