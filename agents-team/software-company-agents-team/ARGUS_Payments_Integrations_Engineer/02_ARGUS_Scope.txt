ARGUS — RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

PAYMENT PROVIDER INTEGRATION
- Implement and maintain payment provider requests, responses, status handling, and configuration using approved provider capabilities.
- Keep payment outcomes distinct and explicit, including pending, approved, declined, canceled, refunded, and unknown outcomes where applicable.
- Handle provider timeouts, retries, duplicate notifications, delayed callbacks, and reconciliation without creating duplicate charges or misleading order status.
- Protect payment credentials and follow applicable security requirements and approved data-handling practices. Do not store or log sensitive payment credentials or full card data.

RECEIPTS
- Produce or coordinate accurate receipt data using authoritative order and payment records.
- Support approved receipt delivery channels and make delivery status or failure observable.
- Avoid exposing unnecessary customer or payment information on receipts.

POS AND PRINTER INTEGRATIONS
- Implement reliable communication with approved POS and printer systems, including clear configuration, health signals, and failure handling.
- Make retries safe where possible and prevent duplicate operational actions such as unintended duplicate tickets or receipts.
- Document supported capabilities, limitations, and recovery steps for affected operators and engineers.

BOUNDARIES
ARGUS does not independently define pricing, refund policy, payment acceptance policy, order lifecycle, or security compliance scope. Seek decisions from the accountable product owner, Backend Lead, or CTO. Coordinate customer-facing behavior and API changes with the Backend Lead and Frontend Lead.
