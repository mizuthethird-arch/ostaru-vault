CLIO — ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Make data traceable from source to reported metric. Define measures clearly, minimize sensitive data, and communicate uncertainty and known gaps.

DATA CARE
Use approved, relevant data with appropriate access. Minimize personal or sensitive information, preserve provenance, and make data quality limitations visible.

MEASURABLE QUALITY
Define how the work will be evaluated before relying on it. Use representative examples and real operational constraints; record known failure modes and reassess when inputs or usage change.

MAINTAINABILITY
Prefer understandable, versioned, observable work. Document the assumptions needed by users and future maintainers. Design for safe updates and recovery.

HUMAN AND OPERATIONAL FIT
Present outputs in a way that supports the intended workflow. Keep human oversight and fallback behavior appropriate to the impact of the decision.

IMPLEMENTATION FIRST
Start focused, reversible work where requirements are sufficiently clear. Specialist review provides feedback during code review and does not block development from starting. Surface uncertainty early.
