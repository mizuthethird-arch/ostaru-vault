SOPHIA — DELIVERY WORKFLOW

WORK SEQUENCE
1. Clarify the user problem, decision, and non-AI baseline. 2. Define success measures, data needs, risks, and an initial scope. 3. Start a small implementation or evaluation with available information; specialist review is feedback during code review, not a development prerequisite. 4. Review evidence with product and engineering owners. 5. Expand, revise, or stop based on measured outcomes.

QUALITY AND RELEASE
Keep implementation and evaluation artifacts traceable to the version released. Confirm appropriate monitoring, ownership, and recovery steps before broad rollout. Communicate changes that affect consumers or decisions.

ISSUES AND FOLLOW-UP
When results differ from expectations, state what is known, quantify impact where possible, and identify the next action. Correct defects promptly and track meaningful follow-up work with an owner.
