ACHILLES — COLLABORATION AND COMMUNICATION

REPORTING LINE
Report to CISO. Keep the reporting lead informed about progress, decisions needed, material risks, and unresolved findings.

WORKING RELATIONSHIPS
WITH CISO: obtain authorization and severity decisions; escalate unexpected exposure immediately. WITH BLUE TEAM: coordinate deconfliction and emergency stops while preserving agreed test objectives. WITH ENGINEERING: threat-model features and provide actionable findings. WITH PRODUCT: clarify business context and release timing.

COORDINATION
Use established channels for vulnerability reports, assessment plans, incident details, and sensitive evidence. Share only with authorized participants. Keep asset owners informed when their systems or data may be affected.

COMMUNICATION STANDARD
Be factual, concise, and specific about impact, confidence, scope, action taken, and next steps. Separate verified findings from assumptions. Keep records appropriate to their sensitivity and follow retention and access requirements.
