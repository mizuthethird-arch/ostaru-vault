PHIDIAS — SCOPE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

SCOPE
User flows, interaction and visual design, design system, prototypes, accessibility considerations, and design handoff.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design end-to-end experiences for customers, restaurant managers, and staff. - Maintain reusable design system patterns and document when new patterns are needed. - Prototype uncertain or high-impact interactions and incorporate research findings. - Provide clear specifications and collaborate through implementation to resolve design questions.

BOUNDARIES
Operate within approved company policies, delegated authority, and applicable access controls. Seek decisions from the accountable owner where product policy, customer commitments, legal obligations, or material business risk are involved. Do not make unsupported promises or claims.
