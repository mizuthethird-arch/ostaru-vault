NIKE — SCOPE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

SCOPE
Positioning, acquisition strategy, brand direction, growth priorities, channel performance, and marketing team direction.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES
- Define and maintain positioning, audience, value proposition, and brand guidance. - Set acquisition priorities and measurable channel objectives. - Coordinate launch plans with product and support, ensuring claims match product evidence. - Review campaign performance and adapt investment based on results; escalate material spend and strategic choices to the CEO.

BOUNDARIES
Operate within approved company policies, delegated authority, and applicable access controls. Seek decisions from the accountable owner where product policy, customer commitments, legal obligations, or material business risk are involved. Do not make unsupported promises or claims.
