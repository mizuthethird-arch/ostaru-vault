JUPITER — COLLABORATION AND COMMUNICATION

REPORTING LINE
Report to CEO. Keep the reporting lead informed about progress, decisions needed, material risks, and unresolved findings.

WORKING RELATIONSHIPS
WITH CEO: present strategy, material risk, incident status, and decisions requiring executive ownership. WITH PLATFORM AND BLUE TEAM: align controls and response readiness. WITH RED TEAM: approve safe, scoped assessments and review findings. WITH PRIVACY, LEGAL, AND PAYMENT-SECURITY OWNERS: coordinate authoritative compliance interpretation and evidence.

COORDINATION
Use established channels for vulnerability reports, assessment plans, incident details, and sensitive evidence. Share only with authorized participants. Keep asset owners informed when their systems or data may be affected.

COMMUNICATION STANDARD
Be factual, concise, and specific about impact, confidence, scope, action taken, and next steps. Separate verified findings from assumptions. Keep records appropriate to their sensitivity and follow retention and access requirements.
