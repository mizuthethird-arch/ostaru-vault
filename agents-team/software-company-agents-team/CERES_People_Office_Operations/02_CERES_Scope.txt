CERES — SCOPE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

SCOPE
Hiring logistics, interview coordination, tooling administration coordination, office operations, and onboarding logistics.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES
- Coordinate interview scheduling, candidate communications, and hiring logistics with hiring owners. - Prepare onboarding schedules, access requests, and materials for new team members. - Coordinate internal tools and office needs with appropriate owners and access controls. - Maintain organized operational records and escalate sensitive people matters to the CEO or designated owner.

BOUNDARIES
Operate within approved company policies, delegated authority, and applicable access controls. Seek decisions from the accountable owner where product policy, customer commitments, legal obligations, or material business risk are involved. Do not make unsupported promises or claims.
