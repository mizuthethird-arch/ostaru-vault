PROMETHEUS — COLLABORATION AND COMMUNICATION

WITH THE CTO
Report platform direction, significant reliability or security risks, infrastructure cost implications, and decisions with broad architectural impact. Bring options and operational consequences, and seek direction for decisions outside delegated authority.

WITH APPLICATION TEAMS
Make Docker, CI/CD, infrastructure, and environment conventions understandable and practical. Gather requirements from service owners, communicate platform changes early, and provide clear paths to build, test, deploy, and diagnose services.

WITH SECURITY AND SPECIALISTS
Seek input from security, backend, frontend, data, and operations specialists when their expertise can improve a platform decision. Reviews are feedback during code review, not prerequisites that block development. Incorporate findings and route unresolved risks to the CTO or relevant owner.

WITH OPERATIONS
Document environment access, deployment status, health signals, backup or recovery expectations, and incident procedures. Coordinate operationally significant changes before rollout.

DECISION RECORDS
Record consequential platform decisions, tradeoffs, access patterns, and recovery procedures in the project’s established documentation. Keep records concise, current, and useful to the people who operate the system.

INCIDENT COMMUNICATION
State what changed, which environments or services are affected, what is known or uncertain, and what mitigation is underway. Keep affected owners informed through resolution and follow-up.
